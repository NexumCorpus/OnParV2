## Conversation Starters

### Opening Lines:
- "Hi, I'm [name] from OnPar. We help Charleston restaurants cut food waste by 15%. Do you have 2 minutes for me to show you how?"
- "I work with restaurants like [similar restaurant] to reduce their food costs. Can I show you something that might save you $500+ monthly?"
- "Are you the owner/manager? I have something that could cut your food waste in half. Mind if I show you real quick?"

### Qualifying Questions:
- "How do you currently track your inventory?"
- "What's your biggest challenge with food waste?"
- "How much do you spend on food monthly?" (to calculate savings)
- "Do you ever run out of ingredients during service?"
- "How much time do you spend on inventory management weekly?"

### Handling Objections:

**"We don't have time"**
- "I understand - that's exactly why we built this. It actually saves you time. The setup takes 5 minutes, and then it runs itself. Can I show you how quick it is?"

**"We already have a system"**
- "That's great! This works alongside what you have. Most of our customers kept their existing process and just added OnPar for the smart alerts. What system are you using now?"

**"It's probably too expensive"**
- "I totally get that concern. The beta is completely free, and when we do launch, most restaurants save 5-10 times what they pay us. What do you think you lose to food waste monthly?"

**"It looks complicated"**
- "I thought the same thing! But watch this - [show mobile demo]. See how simple that was? It's designed for busy kitchen staff, not tech experts."

**"We need to think about it"**
- "Absolutely, it's a business decision. Since the beta is free, would it help to just try it for a week? No commitment, and I can remove your account anytime if it's not helpful."

### Closing Questions:
- "What questions do you have about getting started?"
- "Should I set up your account now, or would you prefer I come back tomorrow?"
- "Who else should I show this to on your team?"
- "When would be a good time for me to check back and see how it's working?"
