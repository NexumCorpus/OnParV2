## 🎯 Presentation Tips

### Before the Meeting:
- **Research the restaurant** (menu, size, location, reviews)
- **Prepare relevant case studies** (similar restaurant type)
- **Charge devices** and test demo
- **Bring business cards** and beta signup materials

### During the Presentation:
- **Start with questions** to understand their pain points
- **Use their restaurant name** throughout the presentation
- **Show, don't tell** - demo the actual product
- **Handle objections immediately** with proof points
- **Ask for the commitment** clearly and directly

### After the Presentation:
- **Send follow-up email** within 2 hours
- **Include beta signup link** and next steps
- **Schedule onboarding call** if they sign up
- **Add to CRM** with notes and follow-up reminders
