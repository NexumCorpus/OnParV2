## Notes for Pitching to Restaurants

### Demo Script Points
1. "This saves restaurants like yours $500+ monthly"
2. "Setup takes 5 minutes, no app downloads needed"
3. "Built specifically for small restaurants in Charleston"
4. "Free beta access, you help us perfect the product"

### Common Objections & Responses
- "We don't have time" → "5-minute setup, works on your phone"
- "Too expensive" → "Free beta, typically saves 5-10x the cost"
- "Too complicated" → "Designed for busy kitchen staff, not tech experts"
- "We have a system" → "Works alongside what you have, just better insights"
