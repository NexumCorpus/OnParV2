"Great! So the problem we solve is simple - most restaurants waste 4-10% of their inventory, which adds up to thousands per year. OnPar uses AI to track your inventory, predict what you'll need, and alert you before things expire.

For example, [Similar Restaurant] was throwing away $300 monthly in expired produce. After using OnPar for 3 weeks, they cut that to under $50. That's $3,000 annual savings.

**"We don't have time for new software"**
"I totally get that. That's exactly why we built OnPar mobile-first. Most updates take 30 seconds on your phone. Mario's Pizza owner does his inventory updates while walking to his car. Would you be open to seeing how simple it is?"

**"We already have a system"**
"That's great - what are you using? [Listen] The thing is, most systems weren't built specifically for restaurants. OnPar was built by restaurant insiders who understand your workflow. Plus, since it's free during beta, there's no risk in trying it alongside what you have."

**"How much does it cost?"**
"Normally it's $49/month, but beta testers get free access during the entire beta period, plus 50% off when we launch. Most restaurants save $500+ monthly, so it pays for itself in the first week."

**"I need to think about it"**
"Absolutely. Can I send you a quick 2-minute video showing exactly how it works? Then you can decide if it's worth a conversation. What's the best email?"

- "Who makes decisions about restaurant software purchases?"

- "What's your biggest inventory management challenge?"
- "How do you currently track what needs to be ordered?"
- "How often do you run out of key ingredients?"
