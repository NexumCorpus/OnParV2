# OnPar Product Overview

OnPar is a smart inventory management SaaS platform designed specifically for small restaurants, cafes, and eateries (1-50 employees). The product helps reduce food waste by 10-20% and save $500+ monthly through intelligent alerts, AI insights, and automated reorder suggestions.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Primary Goal**: Reduce the 4-10% inventory waste that costs the US restaurant industry $162 billion annually
- **Target Market**: Small restaurants, cafes, eateries in Charleston, SC (expanding)
- **Key Benefit**: Cut food waste by 10-20%, saving $500+ monthly per restaurant

## Product Features
- Smart inventory tracking with expiry dates and reorder points
- Intelligent alerts for low stock and expiring items
- Menu performance tracking and waste analysis
- Budget monitoring with overspend alerts
- Mobile-first design for on-the-go management
- AI-powered insights for waste reduction (Premium)
- Recipe cost analysis and profit margin tracking
- CSV import/export for bulk data management

## Business Model
- **Basic Plan**: $49/month - Core inventory management
- **Premium Add-on**: $29/month - Advanced AI insights and analytics
- **Setup Fee**: $299 one-time (includes onboarding and data migration)
- **Current Status**: Beta with 50+ Charleston restaurants

## Key Differentiators
- Built specifically for small restaurants (not enterprise)
- Affordable pricing that pays for itself in first week
- Mobile-first design with no app downloads required
- Charleston, SC based with local market focus
- AI-powered waste reduction recommendations
